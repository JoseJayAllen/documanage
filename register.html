Privacy Policy
1. Introduction

OQAADMS (Office of Quality Assurance & Accreditation Document Management System) is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, institutional email address, employee ID number, and department affiliation.
  • Account Information: Encrypted password, assigned user role (admin, editor, viewer), and account status.
  • System Activity: Documents you upload, download, or manage; timestamps of your actions; and IP addresses for security auditing.
  • Optional Data: Phone number and profile photograph, if voluntarily provided.
3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity and enforcing role-based access controls.
  • Maintaining a complete audit trail for institutional compliance and security.
  • Generating system announcements, weekly reports, and operational updates.
  • Contacting you regarding account activation, password resets, or policy changes.
4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards including bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS encryption for data in transit, and strict access controls. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of two years in accordance with institutional policy. Physical and digital access to servers is restricted to authorized IT personnel only.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage solely to remember your display preferences (such as dark mode) and to cache non-sensitive UI state. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Aggregated, non-identifiable statistics may be shared with institutional stakeholders for reporting purposes.

7. Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access, review, and update your profile information at any time.
  • Request an export of your personal data and document history.
  • Request account deactivation, subject to institutional document-retention policies.
  • Opt out of non-essential system notifications via your profile settings.
8. Data Retention

Account profiles are retained while your account remains active. Inactive accounts may be archived after 24 months. Uploaded documents are governed by the institutional document retention schedule and may be archived separately from account data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or system functionality. Users will be notified of material changes through the system announcement board or via email.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact your system administrator or email office@bsu.edu.ph.
